TempleOS, the real one

This runs the actual TempleOS 5.03 (public domain, by Terry A. Davis) on a pretend 64-bit PC (QEMU) on this computer. It has no network and can't see your files.

Press ▶ Start. It takes about 10 seconds to boot.

It will ask "Install onto hard drive (y or n)?":
 • n: play from the live CD. Nothing is kept when you turn it off.
 • y: install onto this tab's own 2 GB pretend hard drive (it lives in data/vm/templeos). Answer its questions with the defaults, then next time pick "Start from the hard drive".

Click the screen to type and use the mouse. Ctrl+Alt lets go of the mouse. In TempleOS: F1 help · Esc save & exit · Shift+Esc abort · Ctrl+M personal menu · F7 God word
