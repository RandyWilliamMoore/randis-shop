📎 Bindy & Louis

Parody. Original characters, not affiliated with anyone.

Type away. Bindy the paperclip reads along and offers repair tips about whatever you write (phones, laptops, bikes, washers...). Or ask Bindy a repair question in the box under it. Click Bindy for a tip.

Bindy answers from its own tip book, right here in this tab. Tabs can't go online.

Topic:

Move Louis: WASD / arrows. Bindy sticks close to Louis and fixes devices for customers near him, so walk it over to them. Bad guys have a red outline and a red ▼. Throw: click (aims at mouse) or Space (aims at nearest bad guy). Louis blocks shots with his body. Fix devices to open each gate, then push right. M = sound. Volume slider is up top.
