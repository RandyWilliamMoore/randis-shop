Games (ROMs)

Use games you own and copied yourself, free homebrew, or games whose makers let you share them. The emulator suites let you drag a file in or pick one from your computer. Your files go into that site's own browser view. They don't get uploaded to this app.

CHIP-8 keys

The 16 CHIP-8 keys are 1 2 3 4 / Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C V on your keyboard, or click the pad. Click the screen first so the page gets your keys. If a game acts oddly, try the two "Old" switches. Older games expect them.
