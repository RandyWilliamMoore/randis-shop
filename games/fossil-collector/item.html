Places to start if you want real fossils or casts. These come from web search results and the sellers' own descriptions. I did not test any of them. Before buying, ask where and how the fossil was collected, whether it was restored or built from several specimens, and who signed any certificate. Fossil laws differ by country and US state, and a seller's word is not proof.

FossilEra: real fossilsAn online fossil shop. Read its FAQ first.
https://fossilera.com/pages/fossilera-faq
Fossil Realm: real fossilsAn online store with published store policies.
https://fossilrealm.com/pages/store-policies
The Evolution Store: certificates of authenticityExplains what its certificates cover.
https://theevolutionstore.com/certificates-of-authenticity
Bone Clones: castsSays each resin cast is hand-tooled and stained to match the original.
https://boneclones.com/page/why-choose-bone-clones
The Bone Room: museum-quality castsSells casts and fossil plaques.
https://boneroom.com/store/c87/Museum_Quality_Casts.html
Museumize: castsSays its casts come from moulds of authenticated museum specimens.
https://www.museumize.com/collections/fossil-casts-skeletal-reproductions
PBMNH replica catalog: custom workIts catalog lists custom moulding of your own specimens, plus fossil preparation, restoration and mounting. Ask them directly about price and time.
https://www.pbmnh.org/museum-store/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/ReplicacatalognewNov2016.pdf
