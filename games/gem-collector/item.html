    Cutters to test for real versions. A web search found no online ordering service or API for this, so for now this path is a person sending a cutter the brief below and getting a quote. These come from search results and their own descriptions. I did not test any of them. Ask about price, minimums and where the rough comes from. Custom cutting is usually expensive.

    Folk Market Gems: try firstSays it makes custom designs to your specification and takes consultation requests.
    https://www.folkmarketgems.com/pages/gemstone-cutting-services
    Maikurumoto GemsSturbridge, Massachusetts. Its International Gem Society listing says it takes custom commissions from select rough.
    https://www.gemsociety.org/business/maikurumoto-gems/
    Holts LapidaryIts trade page says it can cut and polish a loose stone to any required shape.
    https://www.holtsgems.com/pages/trade-services
    Crystal MythsUK. Offers custom cutting and asks you to get in touch to plan the cut.
    https://crystalmyths.co.uk/lapidary
    International Gem Society faceting diagramsPublished diagrams for real stones. The angles in this game are simplified for play, so a real cutter should use a proper diagram.
    https://main.gemsociety.org/jewelry-lapidary/faceting-diagrams
