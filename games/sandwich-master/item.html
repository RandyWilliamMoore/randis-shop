1. Open the shop and a customer walks up.
2. Take their order and walk to the sandwich station.
3. Bread first, then the fillings. Same order as the ticket = master's bonus.
4. Wrap it, walk back, and serve it over the case before their patience runs out.
5. Earn fame to grow your shop and unlock ingredients. Spend coins on upgrades.
