Space ollie (hold for higher) · also pops off rails and manuals
In the air: ← kickflip · → heelflip · ↓ shove-it · hold ↑ melon grab (let go before landing)
Tap the same arrow twice fast for a double. Finish a flip before you land.
G in the air, then drop onto a rail or ledge to grind. Hold an arrow as you lock on for a different grind.
M manual, keeps a combo alive between landings. ←→ balance grinds and manuals.
C laser flip, only while Special is on. Fill Special with tricks; it doubles combos.

Spend cash from your runs on crates. Each one holds one random part or piece of gear.
