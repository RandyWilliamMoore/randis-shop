Email Terminal

A cheerful retro green-screen email terminal. Type commands to list, read, search, flag, delete, reply and compose mail in a sample inbox, or import your own .eml or .mbox file. New messages save as .eml files or open in your mail app. Connecting a live mail account (IMAP/SMTP) needs Randis UDS.

Use free in your browser

Made with Randis UDS, a free desktop app that builds the tools you describe.
